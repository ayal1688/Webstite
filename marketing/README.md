## Rules on every post

- The narrator is the maker: "I made this." Never a buyer, never a review, never "a friend bought one".
- Mockups say "mockup" on screen. No sample exists yet. Say so if asked.
- TikTok: **Disclose commercial content → Your brand** on anything that shows the shirt, the price or the site.
- TikTok: **AI-generated content ON** for any post with a photoreal mockup. The scripts say which ones.
- TikTok audio: the Commercial Music Library, your own sound, or none. Never a trending song or someone else's sound.
- Facebook Reels: upload the clean file from `videos/`, never a TikTok download. No TikTok library music on Facebook. If Facebook offers an AI label, turn it on.
- Reddit, Facebook groups, forums: the first line of anything that mentions ORU is exactly:
  **Disclosure: this is my own small brand, ORU.**
- No countdowns, no "limited", no discounts, no giveaways, no "tag a friend", no follow-for-follow, no asking anyone to upvote or like.
- Cloth: light 100% polyester, wicks sweat, dries fast. You still sweat, and the printed patch breathes less. Nothing beyond that. If someone asks which blank it is, point to those specs. Don't name a maker. Don't invent a fabric.
- You stay anonymous. No name, no face, no personal details. "One person who rides e-MTB" is enough.

Never post: anything from the retired `ads/ugc-watts` folder, or the `watts-*-4` and `watts-*-5` images. Not even a crop.

## The sample trigger

After the first sale, order one sample. Check the cost in Printify billing first.
It gives you real photos, flat measurements for the product page, a look at the
neck label, a "the real one arrived" post, and later Google Merchant Center.
Until it arrives, everything stays labeled as a mockup.

## What success looks like

- 0–3 sales in 30 days. That's a guess, not a promise.
- A rule of thumb, not verified: 1–2% of visitors buy. That's about 100–300 visits per sale. With no reviews and only mockups, expect the worse end.
- Also success: you know which channel sends people, every account is in good standing, and the first sale pays for the sample.
- Follower counts are not the goal.

## Waiting until the sample exists

Google Merchant Center, Bing, Pinterest catalogs, press pitches, creator commissions, forum promotion.
