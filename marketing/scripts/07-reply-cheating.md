## Trigger

A real comment on an ORU post that says the bike is cheating ("that's cheating", "you're cheating", "ebike = cheating", and so on).

- Open the comment → Reply → the video option → upload the reply file. TikTok adds the comment sticker itself (check in the app for the exact taps).
- Pick a comment about the bike, not about a person. Skip slurs and personal attacks.
- **No real comment yet?** Swap this slot with script 08 and wait. Post 07 in a later slot once a real comment arrives. If none comes by the end of week 3, skip it on TikTok. The no-comment cut is for the Facebook Reel only; don't post it on TikTok on its own. Never write the comment yourself, never use a second account, never make a fake comment sticker.
- **Stitch instead?** Only if a US creator's video asks "is an e-bike cheating?", Stitch is allowed on it, and the app lets you turn the stitched clip's original sound fully off (check in the app). Their part must be silent, with their question shown as on-screen text, and your part must add a real answer. Otherwise skip the Stitch. A Stitch is a new edit, so it needs a re-render: ask Claude at least 2 days ahead. Never download and re-upload their video.
