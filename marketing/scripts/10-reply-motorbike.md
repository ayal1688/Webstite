## Trigger

A real comment on an ORU post like "how is that not a motorbike?", "that's a motorbike", or "just buy a dirt bike". For a comment like "it's not mountain biking", use the swap version.

- Open the comment → Reply → the video option → upload the reply file. TikTok adds the comment sticker itself (check in the app for the exact taps).
- Pick a comment that is about the bike, not a person. Skip slurs and personal attacks.
- **No real comment yet?** Swap this slot with script 11 and wait. Post 10 in a later slot once a real comment arrives. If none comes by the end of week 3, skip it on TikTok. The no-comment cuts are for the Facebook Reel only; don't post them on TikTok on their own. Never write the comment yourself, never use a second account, never make a fake comment sticker.
- **Stitch instead?** Only if a US creator's video asks the same question, Stitch is allowed on it, and the app lets you turn the stitched clip's original sound fully off (check in the app). Their part must be silent, with their question shown as on-screen text, and your part must add a real answer. Otherwise skip the Stitch. A Stitch is a new edit, so it needs a re-render: ask Claude at least 2 days ahead. Never download and re-upload their video.

## Truth check

Beats 2–4 of the motorbike version are only true for a pedal-assist bike with no throttle. If your bike has a throttle, post the swap version files instead, and use the swap caption.

## Owner POV clip (if you have one)

Vertical, from the bars or chest: the cranks turning, then legs stopping and the bike coasting. No face, no reflections, no house or car plates, no other riders' faces, no music playing nearby. Trail sound is fine.

The files in `videos/` already use the fallback and are ready to post. If you have a clip, send it to Claude at least 2 days before the post date for a re-render.
