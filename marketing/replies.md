## 12. When not to reply

- **Someone else's post** where the shirt isn't the topic. Don't bring it up.
- **A second reply** to the same hostile person.
- **Politics, religion, or arguments about where you are.** One line at most on TikTok (section 10). None on Reddit or in groups.
- **Bait, trolls, slurs.** Hide or report. Don't answer.
- **Bots and "we'll get you 10k followers" offers.** Ignore them. Never buy followers, views or likes.
- **When you're annoyed.** Wait an hour.
- **When the only useful answer is a link,** and the sub or group doesn't allow links. On Reddit, never a link in a comment (the one exception, a mod-requested link comment under your own approved post, is in `reddit.md` §4).
- **When you'd have to guess** about fit, feel or durability. Say "don't know yet" or skip it.
- **When someone already gave the right answer.**
- **Never** reply with "link in bio" on TikTok, "go buy it", "share this", "tag a friend", or any request to like, follow or upvote.
