[p] Questions
[h2] Asked before buying
[summary] Can I ride in it?
[p] Yes, for easy laps, dirt jumps and messing about. The cotton-poly
        blend moves sweat better than pure cotton and dries faster. On a long hot
        climb a real jersey still beats it, and we are not going to pretend
        otherwise.
[summary] What does the print mean?
[p] A chart making higher highs and higher lows, which turns into the word
        WATTS, above the words ARE OVERRATED. If you have ever been told your
        bike is cheating, it means something. If not, it is a line going up.
[summary] How does it fit?
[p] Modern retail fit — side seams, tailored through the body and sleeve,
        not boxy. It runs slimmer than a Gildan or Hanes, so if you normally take
        a large in those, take an XL here. Flat measurements for every size are
        on the product page.
[summary] Will the print crack?
[p] Printed direct to garment, so the ink sits in the weave rather than on
        top of it. Cold wash, inside out, hang dry and it outlasts the cotton. A
        hot tumble dryer is what kills a print.
